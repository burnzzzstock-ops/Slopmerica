# Vehicle and citizen rendering

## Verified citizen LOD

The normal `Game.frame` path now calls `PeopleRendererCore.updateLod(camera, q.peopleNear)` before the pedestrian draw lists are rebuilt. At the default 50° FOV the preset distance is preserved. Other perspective FOVs scale the distance by tan(25°) / tan(FOV / 2). Orthographic cameras retain the reference distance.

The fixed-seed hardware check exercises all four presets at 35°, 50° and 110°. On Low, the near ranges are 59.16 m, 40 m and 13.06 m. Six, four and one of seven figures receive near geometry respectively. Every figure stays on exactly one visible list. A distance sweep across the hysteresis band yields near, near, far, far, near. All captured simulated citizen positions, phases and actions stay identical.

The original frozen build fails the first assertion at Low 35°, producing four near figures and a 40 m range. The new build passes all twelve cases. Results are saved in `shots/look/people-fov.json`.

- `peoplelineup.mjs lists` with all four presets passes 20 checks.
- `peoplelineup.mjs feet` passes with worst planted-foot mean slide 9.1% and lowest shoe vertex 4.4 mm from the pavement.
- TypeScript and registry lint pass.

Run lists and feet in separate invocations. Running both modes in one invocation left feet-probe figures in the scene and failed the list expectations.

## Civic normal correction

The civic pack stores XYZ normals in signed int8 records with four-byte stride. The original GPU attribute consumed them at three-byte stride, producing 102,284 wrong vectors among 103,680 vertices and 166 incorrect primitive lengths. An interleaved normal attribute with stride four fixes every vector and length without repacking assets.

`scripts/look/civic-normals.mjs` checks the live THREE attributes against every packed vertex, primitive and LOD. It fails on the original code and passes with zero mismatches after the correction. The before/after sheet `docs/aaa/figures/civic-normal-stride.jpg` shows the bench, transformer and street maple. The sheet was opened and inspected. Material identities, UVs, positions and batch names are preserved.

## Camera-priority vehicle drawing

The normal Game frame supplies the renderer with readonly traffic observations before the existing interpolated draw. Selection runs at 2 Hz, or immediately after a camera move of 30 m or a substantial turn. Cars within a padded image region rank ahead of offscreen cars; distance and 15% retention hysteresis decide the remaining order. Both the preset-wide and existing per-kind limits hold. Low retains its 36/112/930 m LOD distances.

Logical allocation stays intact. Traffic and parking keep every handle, transfer and capacity count. Selected allocated cars use their original model, paint, flags, transform and suspension state. A car whose original allocation returned −1 gets separate bounded CPU presentation state, a local ID-seeded appearance and the same existing GPU model meshes. Its appearance survives eviction and return. No additional vehicle geometry, materials or draw batches are introduced. Its `shown` pose is already interpolated and is consumed once. Only pitch and roll use the same fixed-step alpha once.

Picking uses a new car-ID lookup while retaining the handle API. Street engines, radios, reverse tones, sirens and horns use current drawn IDs; an allocated but unselected car is ineligible, and a selected car with handle −1 is eligible. The camera-local traffic bed and drive-thru intercom use the same membership. Standalone audio clients without handles remain compatible. Current horn voices fade out when their source leaves presentation, as engines and sirens do.

### Exact signal producer gap

For a car that originally received handle −1, Traffic.place currently discards its exact renderer-only signal commands. The presentation observer can read actual pose, braking/damage, validated junction-turn intent and motion-derived reverse. Exact lot/lane blinkers and explicit custom headlight/siren overrides need a producer contract. Existing allocated cars retain all those exact states.

Proposed optional hook, after Traffic.place computes its existing values: `setCarSignals?(id, braking, signal, damaged, reversing)`, plus optional explicit light overrides when produced. Primitive arguments or a reused per-Car record avoid per-step allocation; the record is drawing-only and excluded from saves. No traffic, parking, routing or demand logic was changed for this implementation.
## Further rendering opportunities

The GPU measurements in `PERF.md` identify the scene and water mirror as the largest passes. Lower-detail reflection geometry has direct measured leverage. Pre-baking repeating model geometry and texture variants can move startup construction to the asset build. Packed geometry transforms and normal conversion are suitable for a SIMD-capable offline packer if asset build measurements justify it. The current normal fix directly exposes the existing packed bytes to the GPU and requires no repacking. These opportunities remain separate follow-up work.
