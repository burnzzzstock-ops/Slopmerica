# Review of the look lane's work (ASTRA), 17 to 18 Nov 2026

The owner asked the interface session to review ASTRA's work. This is that review, for the look lane to act on and for the owner to read.

## What was reviewed, and how

- **Scope:** the look lane's own 106 commits since the push began (`232272c..codex/aaa-world` at 8bc9025, merges from other lanes left out): about 6,900 added and 1,070 deleted lines in some 60 source files. Two newer commits on that branch (to 7bc24ee) are not in it.
- **Method:** five independent reads, one per area (audio; roads and vehicles; shadows, level of detail and post-processing; camera, photo mode, water and info views; the world and the seams with the simulation). Each was asked for defects with a concrete scenario, not for style. Then every finding that matters was checked again here, and where it could be, measured in the running game.
- **How sure each finding is** is said on its line:
  - **measured**: seen in the running game on this PC (real GPU), with the numbers given.
  - **read**: checked here by reading the code path at the branch tip.
  - **reviewer**: one reviewer's reading, plausible, not checked again here. Treat it as a lead.

## The short version

Nothing found changes what the simulation computes, draws from its dice, or changes a save. Nothing calls the network or adds a dependency. Disposal, listeners and state restore are in good order almost everywhere. The level of care in the code is high.

What is wrong falls in three groups: a handful of things a player will see, per-frame cost that grows with the town and will be felt first on a phone, and four tests that are red on integration.

Fix first, in this order:

1. Cars that leave in another model's parked car are never drawn (A1).
2. Need bubbles vanish in six info views, fires included (A2).
3. Street wires vanish near water on High, and other translucent materials are at risk (A3).
4. Every level-up ducks the music (A4).
5. The four red tests (C1, C2).

## What was checked and found sound

- **The simulation.** The look lane's commits inside simulation files (`buildings.ts`, `serviceIcons.ts`, `zoning.ts`) touch drawing only. Growth, levelling, the dice, save rows and restore are untouched. The sky reads the simulation's climate, which is seeded, stepped and saved; nothing in the simulation reads the sky.
- **Random streams.** Presentation uses its own generators; `Math.random` is restored in `finally`; nothing draws from a simulation stream.
- **Audio.** Every per-event source has a scheduled stop; the pools allocate nothing per update; no timers, listeners or network calls in `src/audio`; bars are never scheduled in the past; toggles and pre-unlock calls behave; inputs to AudioParams are finite; the master ceiling adds no DC.
- **Disposal.** Post-processing, cascades, god rays, the building clip table, rebuilt road geometry and the water mesh all free what they replace. Per-building and per-car state is keyed by handle and removed on delete, or pruned each scan.
- **Photo mode.** Every listener hangs off one AbortController and is released on exit; enter and exit are idempotent; capture restores the drawing buffer, pixel ratio, targets, viewport and scissor in a `finally`; a null `toBlob` rejects into the toast; the simulation is frozen and nothing is written to it.
- **Camera.** Follow lets go cleanly when its subject is gone; zoom clamps to 25 to 6,500 m; no division by zero in the spring or the pinch.
- **Roads.** The ribbon stitching keeps shared stations on both sides of every edge and preserves winding; draw ranges are restored after each shadow pass; buffers are bounded.
- **Icons.** `hudIcons.ts` is clean: every referenced icon exists by type, unknown ids return an empty string, and the SVG is built from constants only.
